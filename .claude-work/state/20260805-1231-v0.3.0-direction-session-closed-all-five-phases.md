## Key Decisions

**Measure before the document freezes an inference.** Phase 1 probed
`hdf5-temporal-compression` with a falsification test stated in advance
(< 8 bytes/row). The claim **held**, so nothing reopened — but three
unanticipated findings changed §5's wording: szip cannot apply to fixed-width
string columns; the fair comparison is against *compressed* `int64` (1.14–1.27×);
and read time, not size, is the real cost (6.6–15.8×).

**Reposition, not repair; supersede, not rewrite; close, not hold.** The same
principle three times — correct the record in place and keep the evidence, rather
than producing a clean-looking document that destroys what was tried. The held
codegen `goal.md` kept its July body byte-identical under a banner; its hold
marker was archived rather than deleted.

**Codegen restarts fresh.** Maintainer: *"codegen gets its own session, needs a
fresh new look."* It is roadmap R14, naming what to mine from the closed session
(`spike/`, `research/duckdb-driver-jl/reference.md`, `review-decisions.md`) and
the four V1-agnostic undecided findings (8, 10, 13, 15).

**Verification tooling is part of the work, not overhead.** Three of five phases
